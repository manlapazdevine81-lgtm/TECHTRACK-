CSV safety

Files are UTF-8 with a byte-order mark, use CRLF line endings, and quote embedded commas, quotes and newlines. Cells beginning with =, +, -, @, tab or carriage return are neutralized so a spreadsheet cannot execute them. Timestamps are written as ISO-8601 UTC text. Empty rates are left blank (never 0).
