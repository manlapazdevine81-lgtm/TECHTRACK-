About the system

Meet TECHTRACK

Smarter Organization Management. Better Student Engagement.

TECHTRACK is a centralized student organization management and analytics system designed to support the Tech Innovators Society (TechIS) of Batangas State University – The National Engineering University, Lipa Campus.

It brings essential organizational information into one platform, helping authorized officers manage member records, activities, registrations, attendance, and organizational records while providing useful insights into participation, activity performance, and member engagement.

{# ---------------- 4. FEATURES ---------------- #}

What it covers

Everything one organization needs, in one place.

Four capabilities that follow the TechIS workflow — from member records to reports used for planning.

  • Member Management

    Keep member information and organizational records organized in one place.

  • Activity Monitoring

    Manage activities, registrations, attendance, and assigned responsibilities.

  • Data-Driven Insights

    Explore participation trends, attendance rates, and activity performance through analytics.

  • Reports & Dashboards

    Access consolidated summaries and reports to support organizational monitoring and planning.
